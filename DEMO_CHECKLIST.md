## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Camera won't open:**
- Check permissions in Settings > TikTok Trainer
- Restart app
- Check if using physical device (not simulator)

**Notifications not working:**
- Enable in device settings
- Check notification permissions
- Test notification from settings

**Location not detecting:**
- Enable location services
- Check location permission
- Wait 30 seconds for GPS lock

**Recording not saving:**
- Check media library permission
- Ensure sufficient storage
- Check console for errors

**Streak not updating:**
- Check if recording completed
- Verify daily goal settings
- Check progress data in AsyncStorage

## 📝 Q&A Preparation

### Expected Questions

**Q: How is this different from regular camera apps?**
> A: We focus on habit formation, not just recording. The behavioral triggers, streak tracking, and AI learning create lasting behavioral change.

**Q: Does it require internet?**
> A: No, it's completely offline. All data is stored locally for privacy and works anywhere.

**Q: What makes the AI "smart"?**
> A: It analyzes your recording patterns - time, location, frequency - to predict optimal moments and provide personalized insights.

**Q: Is my data private?**
> A: Absolutely. Everything is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

**Q: Can I customize it?**
> A: Yes! Add your own buzzwords, set daily goals, define creative zones, and adjust all notification settings.

**Q: What about battery/storage?**
> A: Minimal impact. Location checks are spaced out, and we only store metadata, not full videos.
